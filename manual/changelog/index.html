Changelog¶

All notable changes to KOLONİ are listed here, newest first. English only.

[Unreleased]¶

[0.1.4] - 2026-11-21¶

Added¶

  • Chat: when the Orchestrator's model has said nothing for 5 minutes in a turn you started (or Qoder has kept its free model queued for 90 seconds), a card asks whether to keep waiting or stop and pick another model, instead of the turn sitting there until you press Stop; like any card, it waits for you on the Ready page, in the Chats list and in a note in the corner (docs).

Changed¶

  • Chat: answers are easier to read at length - larger, airier and softer type, bold words and code without loud colour or a box - and each turn of the Orchestrator's is one card: who answered, with which model and when, its steps as quiet pills and its answer, and once the turn is over a quiet foot with what it did, Copy (the answer as written) and Read aloud, shown when you point at or tab into the card (always, under a turn with nothing else at its foot). Read aloud reads the turn's answer only, no longer each line written on the way. A question the Orchestrator was not let ask stands on its own line ("Wanted to ask: … · not asked"), not folded into the step's line.
  • Chat: while the Orchestrator asks you something, its question waits in that turn's card, whose head says waits for you where its time would be; the closed chat's line says what it asks ("Orchestrator asks: …"), its box on the canvas shows the question's first line when you point at it, and the message box says to answer above or queue a message. A message you queue meanwhile is said to wait once a turn, not once a question; who asks is said once, on the question's card; and once the turn is over its line counts the questions it asked you.
  • Chat: the open chat is a column at the left of the canvas, as tall as it, with no gap around it; drag its right edge (or use the arrow keys on it) to make it 340 to 760 px wide, and the width is kept. It no longer moves about the canvas or changes height; closed, it is the one-line box in the corner as before. Workspace, opened beside the open chat on a window at least 1400 px wide, stands as tall as it too, from under the Teams button; on a narrower one it keeps its height at the bottom, so the canvas keeps room. The model's button under the message box is as wide as its name (docs).
  • Models: while KOLONİ's browser tab is in the background, the page no longer asks every 15 seconds whether the model server answers; it asks once as you come back to the tab.
  • CLIs: GitHub Copilot CLI run Native, on a version whose code was read or measured (1.0.67 and 1.0.105 so far, by the version Copilot reports; after an update of Copilot's, not until that version is measured), works in the agents' scratch folder: a command that starts in a new shell of its own (one that names no shell) is judged in the project folder, so cd .koloni/tmp/...; python a.py runs without a card at Full auto; a delete inside .koloni/tmp/ is still asked there, and on any other version, in a shell it named, or after one that did, the cd is still asked too (docs).
  • Chat, Canvas, Models, Agents, To-do, Memory, Triggers, Usage: nothing you see or do changes. More of the page's code - the chat's messages and message box, rooms, the canvas and its lines, the Models and Usage pages, roles and recipes, To-do, Memory, Knowledge, triggers and the approval cards - was split into smaller parts, each checked to behave exactly as before, so later fixes there are safer.
  • Canvas: in a turn with no team, the members of a discussion or room standing under it in one column join it by one line round the right side of the column, into its right side with an arrow, instead of lines that doubled back behind the cards; an agent waiting for a file held by another in its column is joined the same way. Members' lines have a colour of their own, picking a room lights its members as picking a discussion does, and dots no line uses are no longer drawn (docs).
  • Canvas: the Lead is called the Orchestrator everywhere the canvas names it - on a discussion's or room's card, in their windows, in the side panel, the box of what was said, notes, memory rows, what an agent waits for and the timeline - and a team's lead is its coordinator, as its own box says, on discussion and room cards, in the side panel's discussion, room and wait notes, in what an agent waits for and in the timeline. While a discussion runs, its opener says "Waiting for the discussion" and a member opened for it "Waiting for its turn to speak". A room's card shows its goal, no longer who opened it; discussion and room cards stand in the order they began; words a card cuts show in full on hover; the legend has a row for every line drawn, also in a turn with no team; and a sub-agent you declined says "Rejected", no longer "Not started" (docs).
  • Agents: a speaker in a discussion is told that a speech is at most 1500 characters and that a longer one is cut there (docs).

Fixed¶

  • Ready, Chat: a sub-agent's work kept in its working copy for you to take in (it stopped at its step limit, or its turn broke off) now waits for you everywhere, as it did in its own chat: on the Ready page while that chat is not open, as a mark on the chat in the Chats list, and in a note in the corner (docs).
  • Chat, CLIs: the Orchestrator is now told never to ask you a question in its answer and carry on in the same turn as if you had answered, nor to tell another agent you approved or chose what you did not say: it decides and says what it decided, or, where the decision is yours, asks on a card and waits. It is not told to ask more often. When Codex tries its own way to ask you, KOLONİ declines it and tells Codex to ask on KOLONİ's card, or, where that card cannot be put to you, to decide; run as a session, that no longer counts towards the refusals that stop it (asking that way three times in a row, with none of KOLONİ's tools between, still stops it).
  • Chat, Ready: a question now says who asks - "Orchestrator asks", "Coder asks" - on its card, on the Ready page (no longer "Lead asks"; KOLONİ's own card about an agent, such as its step limit or its work waiting for Take in, says "for Coder") and in the note in the corner ("Orchestrator asks you", no longer "waits for you"; KOLONİ's own card about an agent, such as its step limit, still "waits for you"); a question KOLONİ did not put to you reads "Wanted to ask: … · not asked", with why when you open it (docs).
  • Canvas, Chat, CLIs: a KOLONİ tool call that a CLI agent stopped waiting for - a command, a wait for another team, a message to another agent, a web request - no longer shows as running after the turn: it ends as interrupted, as when you press Stop, also when the CLI cancels it in the middle of its turn (the agent then shows as at work again, not waiting). What it started is stopped as by Stop; a server you let keep running keeps running. After a restart, such a call left running by an earlier turn is closed too. A discussion broken by an error now shows Failed, not Stopped.
  • Models: a model server of your own that has stopped now shows as not answering also when you wrote its model name yourself; before, once its model had answered, KOLONİ kept saying it answered. On Windows the check of a stopped server on this machine takes about a second instead of about 2.
  • CLIs: Qoder's Native way of running now says what was measured, on the Models page and in its agent's window: the Qoder versions tried so far do not apply KOLONİ's hook, so a native turn stops before Qoder does anything and says to run it as a session. It used to say that Qoder asks before every tool, not yet measured, and that one tool could run before the run stopped (docs).
  • Chat: on the card that asks which candidate's work goes on, a candidate that stopped before its end is said to have "stopped before its end", no longer that "nobody is watching this turn".
  • Canvas: lines that were missing, crooked or pointing the wrong way are fixed: talk between the Orchestrator and a sub-agent it opened has a 💬 you can click; your message's line down to its Plan is short and straight; a folded box of five or more files keeps its lines; findings handed out of a team's frame get their line; the Orchestrator's lines go straight into the teams' doors; a member's line to a discussion between teams lands on its dot, leaves a folded frame by its side and no longer runs through an approved plan; arrows inside a team's frame point at the right end; and in a compare turn the second Everyone card stands beside its own Orchestrator.
  • Canvas: a paused room no longer looks busy: its card loses its "at work" ring, the lines of the members it holds stop moving (one still finishing its step keeps its moving line), and such a member says "Paused with the room" (docs).
  • Workspace: a 3D model you have not turned yet stays seen whole when its window grows, shrinks or is still taking its size as it opens; before, it kept the view framed for the window's first size.

Security¶

  • Chat: a PDF attached to a chat is read with pypdf 6.21.4, which fixes known flaws in reading crafted PDF files.
  • Canvas: a chart in a PowerPoint file shown in the Office view is drawn with Apache ECharts 6.1, which fixes a cross-site scripting flaw; the page's build and test tools (Vite, and undici under jsdom) are updated past their known flaws too.

[0.1.3] - 2026-11-20¶

Changed¶

  • Canvas, Chat, Agents, Models, Settings: nothing you see or do changes. The page's code that draws the canvas, follows the chat's events, summarises tools, rooms, commands and triggers, reads pasted text and attachments, and shows the cards, the Models, Agents, To-do, Board, Triggers and Settings pages was split into smaller parts, each checked to behave exactly as before, so later fixes there are safer.

Fixed¶

  • Canvas, Chat: a discussion whose Orchestrator gave up waiting for it (a CLI that stops a tool call it has waited on too long), or that broke off with an error, no longer shows as running for good: it ends as Stopped (one given up on also has its call shown as interrupted, as when you press Stop).

[0.1.2] - 2026-11-20¶

Changed¶

  • Agents, Settings, Models: nothing you see or do changes. The code behind an agent's turn, taking work in, teams' recipes, rooms, discussions, the Orchestrator's answers in your place, and the chat, project and provider settings was split into smaller parts, each checked to behave exactly as before, so later fixes there are safer.

[0.1.1] - 2026-11-14¶

Added¶

  • Install: KOLONİ copies its database at its first start of a day to runtime/data/backups/ and keeps the last three, beside the copies it makes before an update changes the database (docs).

Changed¶

  • Chat: the Orchestrator no longer stops at a fixed count of steps; it stops when its work is done, when it goes round in circles (it is told once to try another way; then a card asks you Go on or Stop here, tells you on the desktop and at the project's notification address, and ends the turn with its work kept if nobody answers in 10 minutes), or at a limit you set on the Lead role or a trigger. Steps an agent may take now holds sub-agents (docs).
  • To-do, Triggers: in a run nobody watches, an Orchestrator going round in circles ends with the reason on record, and a night or the To-do run goes on with the next job or task; such a turn ends once nothing has moved for Stuck after this many minutes with no progress (Settings › The app, 30 unless you change it), and a To-do task's turn or a night's job after An unattended turn runs at most (hours) (8; empty: no limit) - a night's job no longer stops at 60 minutes (docs).
  • Canvas: where the default provider isn't answering and the Models page holds another one that is switched on, First steps offers Use … for new chats, which makes that one the default; the step is done once the default answers (docs).
  • To-do, Tools, Knowledge: an empty list of tasks, skills or knowledge says what one is and where on the page to add the first; a page that needs a folder, in a chat with none, has a Choose a folder button.
  • Knowledge: in a project whose Project notes are first made from this version on, the Orchestrator uses them from the first note you add (it is told where their guide is and can look them up) until you switch them off on the Lead role's Knowledge tab; other roles have them only if you switch them on. A project that already has Project notes is not changed: switch them on once on the Agents page, the Lead role, Knowledge tab. An Orchestrator with any knowledge on Map is now told where its guide is, as a sub-agent is (docs).
  • CLIs: when the context of an Orchestrator on a CLI fills during a turn in a chat you are in and the CLI's own error says so (GitHub Copilot CLI's wordings: one seen in a live refusal, the others taken from its package; Claude Code's and Qoder's are not recognised yet), KOLONİ summarises its earlier work and starts the turn again from the summary by itself, once, with a line in the chat saying so; if it fills again, the turn fails and says what to do. Where nothing of its earlier work is left to summarise (a single message larger than its model takes), nothing is started again and the failure says so. Where it had opened sub-agents, a room or a team's recipe, the turn is not started again: its earlier work is summarised after it, and your next message goes on from the summary. A CLI lead with no context window on its role can also be summarised after a turn against the catalogue's window for its model, which only a very long chat reaches (docs).

Fixed¶

  • Chat: an agent's work inside its scratch folder, .koloni/tmp/, no longer raises a card at every permission level: a command run in a folder there (or one that starts with a plain cd .koloni/tmp/...) is judged as any other, and deleting a folder of the scratch (rm -rf .koloni/tmp/out, Remove-Item -Recurse) runs unasked at Full auto as deleting a file there does. A line that then leaves the scratch, looks up where it is, runs git there or deletes through a link is still asked; so are a cd into the scratch by a CLI that does not say where its commands run and may keep the folder (Qoder run Native, Claude Code's PowerShell and Monitor tools and its own sub-agents, GitHub Copilot CLI, agy; docs), and deleting the browser's own folder there (.koloni/tmp/browser).
  • Ready: a team whose coordinator KOLONİ stopped while one of its agents was still at work keeps running until that agent is back, and its work is offered at Ready for you; Ready for you is also asked before the Orchestrator ends its turn where nobody asked it earlier.
  • Models: with a model server that is not running, KOLONİ's check of it takes about half as long (on Windows about 2 seconds instead of 4): a server that takes no connection is asked once, not twice.

  • Chat: under the Orchestrator's last answer of a turn that changed files, KOLONİ says from its own records where the work is: in the project, waiting in Ready for you, or nowhere (docs).
  • Chat: work that was open when KOLONİ stopped is marked interrupted, and nothing is run again by itself (docs).
  • Chat: while you're away, the Orchestrator plans jobs on a card; each runs in its own copy after your Start, and you decide what comes in when you are back (docs).
  • Chat: work while you're away has a stop time and a spend limit only if you set them; the project's monthly budget still holds.
  • Chat: Compare sends one message to two models side by side, and you keep the work of one (docs).
  • Chat: attach files, screenshots and videos (sent as frames), and save attachments into the project (docs).
  • Chat: a long context is summarised past Summarise a long context at (%), for API models and most CLI modes; the context button shows how full it is, with Compact now (docs).
  • Chat: slash commands of your own, a command palette on Ctrl+K, and an agent's dev server shown as a preview, on this machine only (docs).
  • Chat: dictation and answers read aloud (docs).
  • Canvas: each turn is drawn from its record: every agent a box, with lines for work handed on, talk, findings and waiting; a legend explains every line and sign (docs).
  • Canvas: teams in frames, rooms and discussions between teams in a lane of their own, and a step's earlier rounds folded into one line each.

  • Agents: ready roles (Orchestrator, Planner, Coder, Reviewer, Tester, Product owner and more) and your own, each with its tools and skills (docs).
  • Agents: three ready roles for a website, in the Web app team: SEO specialist, Copywriter and Accessibility reviewer (docs).
  • Agents: a role's Model cards: which model, how hard it thinks (Auto lets the agent that opens it choose), and how much it reads.
  • Agents: teams with steps, picked per chat; a team can wait for another one (docs).
  • Agents: recipes with steps, loops, branches and conditions, drawn, copied or described in a sentence; the ready ones include Feature, Bugfix, Review only and Evolve (docs).
  • Agents: candidates: one job given to 2 to 5 agents, each scored by your measure command; a card lists the scores and you pick, and at Full auto the best goes on by itself (docs).
  • Agents: rooms: two to six agents on one goal at once, with a budget, Pause, Resume and End (docs).
  • Agents: a room an agent opens gets at least 60,000 tokens, so a small budget no longer ends it before anyone posts. Change the number under Tokens, at least in Settings › This project › New chats start with; the most still wins. Existing projects and chats get 60,000 too (docs).
  • Agents: discussions: agents talk one question through in rounds, and you can join in (docs).
  • Agents: reviewers end with findings that go to the role they name; the Observer, when on, says what each sub-agent's record shows (docs).
  • Agents: tell an agent something while it works; at its step limit a card offers Continue (+25 steps) or Stop here, and Steps an agent may take sets the limit (docs).
  • Agents: when you are away, the Orchestrator answers the safe questions, if you switch it on (Settings › This project › Asking and away), and shows them under Decided for you (docs).
  • Models: an OpenAI-compatible server with tool calls, such as llama.cpp's llama-server or a hosted API, the Anthropic API, and CLIs, side by side; each agent shows the provider and model it really ran on (docs).

  • Knowledge: Add KOLONİ’s own guide makes this guide a knowledge your roles can be given (docs).
  • Knowledge: after a turn, Add to Project notes? keeps fixed findings and docs changes in a Project notes knowledge (docs).
  • Tools: built-in tools by group (files, commands, web, a browser on the sites you allow, team, To-do, memory), given to each role on its Tools tab; five can be switched off for every agent (docs).
  • Tools: MCP servers; What does it do? looks inside an npx or uvx package before you add it (docs).
  • Tools: skills from a folder or GitHub, given to roles, with a record of how each did (docs).
  • Usage: tokens and known cost by turn, day and month, and a monthly budget per project (docs).
  • Usage: plan meters for Claude Code, Codex, agy and Copilot, from what each CLI last reported (docs).
  • Settings: one place in three levels, This chat, This project and The app, with a short sheet for the chat under the message box (docs).
  • Settings: a folder's rules and run settings live in the folder and go into git; settings from another machine are asked about before they are used (Use them / Not now) (docs).
  • Settings: Export everything and Restore from a file… (docs).
  • Settings: notifications to a webhook (Slack, Discord, ntfy), a desktop notification on Windows only, and a daily summary (docs).
  • Settings: Delete project… removes KOLONİ's record of a project, never its folder (docs).
  • Install: Python 3.12 to 3.14 on Windows (tested daily) and Linux (automatic tests, less daily use); macOS not tested yet; a Docker image, the newest and least tested (docs).
  • Install: the update scripts back up the database, update, and rebuild the guide where Zensical is installed (docs).
  • Install: licensed AGPL-3.0-or-later (docs), with a commercial license for those who cannot meet its terms (docs).
  • Docs: a user guide in English, published on GitHub Pages and, after one build with the optional Zensical package, served by KOLONİ itself at /guide/, offline and matching the version you run; hints link to it with Learn more → (docs).
  • Docs: pictures of the main screens, short videos, diagrams, and pages for KOLONİ in 5 minutes, Which way to work, troubleshooting and known limits (docs).

Security¶

  • Install: KOLONİ listens on 127.0.0.1 only and sends no telemetry; every request to its API needs the page key, so a plain or injected command an agent runs cannot approve its own cards (docs).
  • Install: exports and backups are downloaded without the page key in their address, so it stays out of your browser's history.
  • Settings: an export without keys also leaves out secret files you attached to a chat (a .env, a key file); the chat keeps their name (docs).
  • Install: a link with a wrong key, from a web page or an agent's answer, no longer locks the page out: the page keeps its key unless KOLONİ accepts the new one, and links in agents' answers and diagrams open in a new tab.
  • Ready: reading secret files, writing most files that steer agents, and commands that delete or send data off the machine stop at a card at every level, with a few exceptions for CLIs run with their own tools (limits); at Full auto these checks are for honest commands, not a wall (docs).
  • Ready: a command an agent runs is judged together with the folder it runs in, so a relative path inside .claude or another protected folder stops at a card as if it were named; Claude Code and Qoder don't say where their shell stands, so their commands are judged at the project root (a cd into a protected folder is itself a card).
  • Ready: KOLONİ's own cards have fixed titles and say KOLONİ asks; an agent's question says The agent asks, so it cannot pass for one of KOLONİ's.
  • Workspace: sub-agents work in working copies, not in your folder, until you take their work in.
  • Agents: your home folder and a drive's root cannot be a project, and KOLONİ's own folder is never opened, even inside a project; a CLI's own Grep and Glob cannot search it either (docs).
  • Agents: web pages, search results, MCP results, knowledge, a GitHub issue and a learned repository's code reach agents framed as data, not instructions; the frame tells the model, it is not a wall; text that tries to give orders shows a Text tried to give orders card (docs).
  • Settings: secret files open only on your Allow once and searches skip them (at Full auto a command that doesn't name the file can still read it); keys in command output show as ***; variables with secret names are kept from commands and CLIs unless you name them (docs).
  • Settings: a planted test key stops a command, web request, browser or MCP call that would send it out.
  • Chat: an agent asks for a key on a Give a secret to its commands? card; the value never reaches the model or the record, and never leaves on a branch (docs).
  • Triggers: turns nobody watches may do only what the trigger allows, and never start a CLI that works with its own tools (docs).
  • Tools: commands can run in a fresh Docker container, with the network off unless you allow it (docs).
  • Tools: MCP servers get only their own variables and what programs need to run, none of KOLONİ's or a CLI's sign-in; one you have not trusted asks before every call (docs).
  • Tools: web searches and requests reach only public addresses; the agents' browser opens only previews and the sites you allow.
  • CLIs: on Windows, a CLI and everything it starts run in a Windows job that is ended on Stop or when KOLONİ closes.
  • Docs: the guide at /guide/ loads its scripts, styles, fonts and pictures only from KOLONİ itself.
  • Docs: report a security problem privately through GitHub's Security tab (docs).
