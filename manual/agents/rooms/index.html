Rooms¶

A room is two to six agents working on one goal at the same time, in one working copy. They share the work out first, and each file has one holder. What one of them posts, the others read at their next step.

Opening a room¶

Under More next to the message box, choose Work together… ("Open a room: two to six agents working on one goal at the same time"). Then:

  • Goal: what the room should do.
  • Members: a role for each, and its model. It starts with a Coder and a Reviewer. Add a member and Remove change the list.
  • What it may spend: posts by its members, tokens sent and written, and minutes. Empty means no limit. The room ends at the first limit it reaches.
  • These are this room's own. The project's default limits have one place: Settings › This project › New chats start with.

Press Open the room. The Orchestrator, a coordinator, or a recipe's Room part can open a room too. A room an agent opens gets at least 60,000 tokens, even if the agent asks for fewer. One member's step, with what it rereads, takes about 8,000 to 9,000 tokens, so a smaller budget would end the room before anyone posts. Tokens, at least in the same settings changes this number. If it is higher than Tokens, at most, the room gets the most.

Each project's default limits are under Settings › This project › New chats start with. A chat with no folder has no token limit for its rooms unless you set one when you open the room.

The room window¶

The window is titled "Room · goal". It shows how much of its budget is used, the room's items, what was said, the reports, open questions, and what waits for you.

  • Post writes to the room.
  • Give it more adds posts, tokens or minutes to its budget.
  • Pause and Resume hold it and let it go on.
  • End ends it.
  • Let the Orchestrator follow: on, the Orchestrator reads what is said in the room, and may post, ask one more in, or end the room. Your words, Pause and End still come first. Off, it takes no part and is given the room's record when it is over.

A minimised room shows as a tab, "Room · goal · N posts".
