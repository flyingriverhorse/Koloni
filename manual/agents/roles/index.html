Roles¶

A role is what an agent is for: a description, instructions, the tools it may use and, if you like, its own model. Open Agents on the left rail ("Who does the work, in which teams, by which recipes"). The page has three tabs: Teams & roles, Recipes and History.

The roles¶

The Orchestrator is the agent you talk to. It opens other roles as sub-agents when a part of the work needs them. KOLONİ comes with these roles:

Orchestrator, Planner, Coder, Tester, Reviewer, Researcher, Debugger, Architect, Security reviewer, Docs writer, Refactorer, UI designer, Data analyst, DevOps, Spec writer, Performance engineer, Database engineer, Dependency keeper, Translator, Summarizer, Product owner, Assistant product owner, Observer, Data engineer, ML engineer, Data scientist, Model evaluator, MLOps engineer, Designer, SEO specialist, Copywriter, Accessibility reviewer.

The Roles list on the Teams & roles tab shows them all. Filter roles… searches it, and the buttons All, In no team and Off narrow it. Each role has an on / off switch. The Orchestrator, the Planner and the Observer are always on.

A role's panel¶

Click a role to open its panel. It has four tabs:

  • Overview: its category, the teams it is in, what it may do, and its Model as four answers: Which model, How hard it thinks, How much it reads and Who may move it (and Runs as where a role can run inside the CLI). Click an answer, or Edit, to change it.
  • Instructions: what the role is told.
  • Tools: the tools it may use, grouped (Files, Commands, Web, Browser, Team, To-do, Memory). A click switches a tool on or off and saves at once. A role needs at least one tool. Tools marked "asks" wait for your yes, as the chat's permission level says. Below, the MCP servers and skills the role has; you change those on the Tools page.
  • Knowledge: a switch for each Knowledge topic. On, choose Full ("its whole guide at each step") or Map ("its guide, read when its work touches it").

Editing a role¶

Edit opens the role's form:

  • Name, and What it does: "The Orchestrator reads it to pick the role."
  • Category and Instructions.
  • Model: a card for each question. Each says one line; Learn more holds the rest.

Which model asks Who picks it?, then only what that choice needs:

Choice What it needs What it means
Orchestrator From and to "It picks a tier for each job, inside this range, and says why."
As the chat nothing "Whatever the chat's model picker says."
One tier Fast, Balanced or Strongest "Always this tier. The Orchestrator can't change it."
One model a Provider and a Model name "Always this model, whatever the job."

The Orchestrator itself has no Orchestrator choice. Try first lists up to 5 models from the team guide, tried in order before the choice above; + Add a model adds one. See Tiers.

How hard it thinks (Thinking) is Auto or One fixed level, and one ladder of the levels Off to Max:

  • Auto: the agent that opens a sub-agent picks a level for each task: Low for reading or summarizing, Medium for normal code, High for hard bugs or design. The levels it may pick are shaded, from Low up to the one marked most (High unless you move it, or the nearest lower level a model without High takes); click a level to move it. Its pick, and its reason, show under Details in the agent's window. Auto never picks Off. If it picks nothing, KOLONİ sends Medium (or the most, where that is lower), and the agent's window says "Thinking Medium, KOLONİ's default for Auto (the opener gave no level)".
  • One fixed level: every task gets the level you click, any level its model takes; the Orchestrator doesn't change it.

An agy model whose name ends in its level, such as gemini-3.8-flash-low, takes no other: it is sent none, and the agent's window says so. Under How hard it thinks, the role's Model card says "The model's own" and "its name sets its level: none is sent".

A role from before may still have a most next to a fixed level, or a most of Off under Auto. A line under the ladder says so ("The Orchestrator may also go up to High (set earlier)"), and Remove takes it away.

Struck levels are ones the role's model does not take, named under the ladder. A model without a level gets the nearest lower level it takes, never down to Off; one whose levels are not known gets none. Back to Auto, a most of Off, or one the model is known not to take, is taken away.

Nobody opens the Orchestrator, so it has no most: a click sets its level, and with Auto no level of its own is sent, and the chat's level, or the model's own, applies.

How much it reads (the context window) says the window found for its model, and where from. Past the % set in Settings › The app ("Summarise a long context at (%)") KOLONİ summarizes the older part, unless its CLI keeps its own session and does that itself. Where the window is unknown there is no fullness meter and nothing is summarized early. Set by hand takes a window of your own.

Who may move it: Any provider in the team guide, Only these, or No one. An agent that moves a sub-agent to another provider must say why.

Run inside the CLI: only when the agent that opens it runs in Claude Code. Cheaper, and counts toward no turn or role limit; it is not its own agent on the canvas, stops with the agent that opened it, and is never used while you're away or for a trigger. Not for the Orchestrator, the Observer or a team's lead. - It may: Read and search, Write code, Run commands, Use the web, or Pick tools one by one. - This role will sums up what the role will do, as you change the form.

Press Save. A built-in role has Reset to default; your own has Delete role.

A new role¶

New role (on the Teams & roles tab) opens a form. "Agents in this role are opened by the Orchestrator or a team lead." Start from:

  • Blank;
  • An expert: it reads only; an expert changes nothing;
  • A copy of another role: it gets that role's servers, skills and knowledge, but not its teams.

Press Create role. An agent can also propose a role while it works; you decide on the Create this role? card.

History¶

The History tab shows what the roles, teams and recipes did in the Last 30 days or Last 7 days: how many agents were opened and ended done, and the team and recipe runs. Filter by role, team or recipe, or tick Failed only. Each row shows when, the role, the chat, the model and how it ended; click the chat to open it.
