Upgrade notes¶

What happens when you upgrade¶

  1. Back up first. The update script backs the database up to runtime/data/backups/ before it pulls the new version. For more, use Settings › The app › Your data › Export everything.
  2. Update: stop KOLONİ, then run ./scripts/update.ps1 (Windows) or ./scripts/update.sh. See Backup and update.
  3. Start KOLONİ again. If the new version changes the database, KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/ and then upgrades it. It keeps the last five copies there, the update script's included; the copies it makes at the first start of a day (daily-…, the last three) are apart from those.

If something goes wrong, the copies in runtime/data/backups/ are whole databases. Stop KOLONİ before you put one back in place of runtime/data/koloni.db.

Version by version¶

Only what needs your attention after an update; everything else is in the Changelog.

0.1.11¶

At its first start the database is upgraded by two migrations, 0197 and 0198, after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/. Neither deletes anything of yours.

Migration What it does to your data
0197 A table for the decisions the Orchestrator notes for you under Decided for you (note_decision). Going back drops it.
0198 Each chat keeps when you last looked at it ("Done · not seen"). Every chat there is at the upgrade counts as seen. Going back drops the column.
  • The Orchestrator adds preferences from your words. New preferences from your words (Settings › The app › Agents) starts at Add them, tell me: a plain rule you state ("from now on write commits in Turkish") goes into Your preferences at once, and the chat says so with Undo. Choose Ask me first to have each one on a card (see Who writes what).
  • Check what I said first runs fewer commands. Where its box works, a line of your preferences no longer lets a command run - it can only stop one -, and an earlier turn's message no longer runs a push, a pull request or a merge: of your messages only this turn's do, Send now included (this project's rules still can). Such a command waits on its card (see Check what I said first).
  • At Full auto the judge decides a plain push of your feature branch. In a chat you watch at Full auto with the judge on, a plain push of the branch you have checked out to its own origin goes to the judge instead of a card: fine pushes it, risky gives its card - never a refusal. A push to main, a force push and any other push keep their cards (see A push of your feature branch). The judge reads new words, "D2" (see Full auto's judge).
  • More files ask at every level. Writing .devcontainer/ or .devcontainer.json, .gitmodules, .cargo/, .mvn/, Gradle's wrapper, .bazelrc or .bazelversion asks at every level, Full auto too, and so does a git remote that changes origin (add, set-url, remove, rename, set-head, set-branches). Where an agent did this unasked at Full auto, it now waits on a card (see Files that steer later turns).

0.1.10¶

0.1.10 makes KOLONİ smaller: Board, triggers, the Observer, the Office and 3D previews, rooms, the notification address (Slack, Discord), the search by meaning, Usage's Cards and Codex's One run per model step are gone, and eight of the eleven ready teams. At its first start the database is upgraded by eight migrations, 0189 to 0196, after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/. Copy runtime/data/koloni.db yourself before you update too (with KOLONİ stopped): what these migrations delete - your triggers, the Observer's role and its observations, the notification address, the search by meaning's vectors, the eight ready teams - comes back only with such a copy, put back in place of runtime/data/koloni.db under 0.1.9.

Migration What it does to your data (each is told below)
0189 Triggers are gone: your triggers and their records are deleted.
0190 The Observer is gone: its role, its switch and the observations on agents are deleted.
0191 The notification address is gone: deleted.
0192 The search by meaning is gone: its vectors and the model chosen for it are deleted.
0193 Rooms are gone: open_room is taken off every role, a recipe's Room step becomes an All at once group.
0194 Three ready teams: the other eight go where they are as they came.
0195 Feature, Model change and Pipeline change run fewer agents, where they are as KOLONİ made them.
0196 Project notes are Memory's: their lines are copied into Memory; a To-do task of no story gets a notebook.
  • New worktrees and copies go into your project's .koloni/. A branch's worktree (a ☾ Away job's, a team's on a branch) is made in .koloni/worktrees/, and in a project with no git a sub-agent's copy in .koloni/copies/; in a project in git copies stay outside it, as before. The first worktree adds /.koloni/worktrees/ and /.koloni/copies/ to the repository's .git/info/exclude, so git status stays clean. Copies and worktrees kept from before stay where they were made and are decided there (see Where copies and worktrees are). Nothing in the database changes.
  • KOLONI_TRIGGER_CLOCK is now KOLONI_CLOCK. A .env that sets the old name keeps working for one more release; rename it to KOLONI_CLOCK (see Environment variables).
  • Settings' sections have new names. This project's eleven sections are seven, by job: How agents work (what was Agents, New chats start with and Slash commands), When you're asked (Asking and away, Runs without asking - its rules behind Show the rules - and Commands and secrets), When the work is done (Checks and git), then Limits and money, Memory and web, Notifications and This folder as they were. No setting is gone: the ones few change, Docker and the secret variables among them, are under a section's More. Ctrl+K and the links still find a section by its old name. Nothing in the database changes (see Settings).
  • Full auto's judge with no model to ask makes cards. At Full auto, where no Judge model can be asked and the Fast tier has none, each command the judge would look at now waits for you on a card - it ran unasked before. Fill the Fast tier on the Models page, or turn the judge off for the project to have Full auto as it was before the judge (see Full auto's judge).
  • At Full auto the judge decides a plain delete in the project. In a chat you watch at Full auto with the judge on, a delete or an emptying in the project, git work thrown away and a stop by name, written as a plain line, go to the judge instead of a card: fine runs, risky is refused (or its card, with Ask me). Whatever reaches out of the project, a line that is not plain, and every level below Full auto keep their cards; a git remote's branches overwritten or deleted (git push --force) wait on a card at every level, whatever your words say (see What the judge decides in place of the list).
  • Native is experimental. Claude Code, Codex and Copilot offer Native under Experimental in How it runs only with Show experimental features on (Settings › The app › Agents; the switch that was Show experimental CLIs, kept as you had it). A provider you already run Native keeps working as before, its card tagged Experimental. Nothing in the database changes.
  • Pages and A test for the change are off unless ticked. In Settings › This project › When the work is done › More › Check by kind of work, a project that never changed Pages has it off from now on, and A test for the change is a new box, off. A turn that wrote a page says "Not checked: pages switched off for this project", and one that changed code and no test says "Not checked: a test for the change switched off for this project". Tick them to have them as before (see What KOLONİ checked). Nothing in the database changes.
  • Project notes are Memory's. At the first start the database is upgraded (migration 0196), after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/: where the Orchestrator had a project's Project notes on (Agents › Lead › Knowledge), their lines, as the folder's .koloni/knowledge/project-notes/learned.json lists them, are copied into the second box under This project's rules in Memory's Your words - the newest that fit in 4 000 characters. Notes you had switched off, or never switched on, are not copied. The folder is only read; the knowledge and its pages stay in the database and in the folder, but no role is given it and the Knowledge page no longer lists it. Lines not copied are still in the folder's pages/: add them in the box if you want them. From now on the box is the switch: what is in it, the Orchestrator is given every turn. A To-do task of no story gets a notebook of its own (empty). Going back to 0.1.9 drops both, and 0.1.9 has its Project notes knowledge as it was: Project notes you keep and tasks' notebooks written after the upgrade go with them (the copy in runtime/data/backups/ is the database as it was before the upgrade, without them).
  • Knowledge is the Memory page's second part. The rail's Knowledge is gone: press Memory, then its tab Sources to look things up in (or Ctrl+K, Knowledge). Agents look things up with Recall, which also reads the pages of a role's knowledge; a role, recipe or prompt of yours that names lookup keeps working in 0.1.10 - name recall instead before the next release. Nothing in the database changes.
  • The Board page is gone. How each chat stands is on the Chats list, what waits for you on Ready for you, and a file two chats want still shows above both of them. Its Re-run the ones that failed button went with it: ask the chat's Orchestrator to open those sub-agents again. Nothing in the database changes.
  • Ready is Work, with To-do in it. The rail's Ready is now Work, and its To-do is gone: Work has two tabs, Ready for you and To-do, each story on To-do a card that opens in a panel (Ctrl+K's Work › To-do and a chat's Open it in To-do bring you to that tab). What waits for your word on To-do is in Ready for you's one list; the rough note's button is Split into tasks. Nothing in the database changes (see To-do).
  • To-do has no goal level. Your stories stand at the top of the tree, those a goal held too; a story's ⋯ no longer offers Goal, and the Hive keeper writes a note as stories and tasks only. Your goals are not deleted: they stay in the database, and nothing in it changes.
  • Three ready teams. At the first start the database is upgraded (migration 0194), after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/: of the ready teams only Web app, Quality and Docs stay as they were. Backend / API, Data, DevOps, Research, Product, Maintenance, ML and DataOps go where they are as they came, with their stages and coordinators; one you changed or use (picked in a chat in Manual, kept for a project's new chats, named by a recipe's step) stays as your own team. Every role and recipe stays. Fourteen built-in roles are now in no ready team (Architect, Database engineer, Performance engineer, Data analyst, Researcher, DevOps, Dependency keeper, Hive keeper, Scout, Data scientist, ML engineer, Model evaluator, MLOps engineer and Data engineer). The Orchestrator still opens them where the work needs one: with Orchestrator decides the teams it picks are where it starts, not a fence, and any built-in role opens beside them (not while a recipe's step holds it to its roles). In Manual it reaches one only when you name it, a recipe's step names it, or you put it in one of the chat's teams (Which roles the Orchestrator opens). Going back to 0.1.9 does not bring the eight teams back: only that copy does - stop KOLONİ, return to 0.1.9 and put the copy back in place of runtime/data/koloni.db.
  • The Word, Excel, PowerPoint and 3D model previews are gone. Such a file is still in the Workspace's files and opens there as any other file that is not a page or a picture: its text where it is text of 256 KB at most (an OBJ, a text STL, a glTF), else "Not text: there is no code to show." or "Too large to show: over 256 KB." - one only in an agent's working copy so far is read there, as its preview was. Open it in its own program from the project's folder. A Word, Excel or PowerPoint file an agent made is still listed among its outputs on the canvas and still checked; a 3D model is no longer an output, and an agent can no longer show one. Nothing in the database changes.
  • The Observer is gone. At the first start the database is upgraded (migration 0190), after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/: the built-in Observer role, the switch Have a model look over each sub-agent's report and the observations kept on agents are deleted, and an agent's window no longer shows one. What the Orchestrator was told then stays in its chat. A role of your own named "Observer" stays as it is. What a sub-agent's record shows is in Checked and No file changed (see What KOLONİ checked). To go back to 0.1.9, stop KOLONİ, return to 0.1.9 and put that copy back in place of runtime/data/koloni.db.
  • Triggers are gone. At the first start the database is upgraded (migration 0189), after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/: your triggers, their runs, their record and the triggers agents proposed are deleted, and Propose triggers is taken off every role, your own too. The turns triggers started stay in their chats as they were. What still starts by itself: ☾ Away's jobs, a chat's queue of To-do tasks and the day's summary. To go back to 0.1.9, stop KOLONİ, return to 0.1.9 and put that copy back in place of runtime/data/koloni.db; your triggers come back with it.
  • Three ready recipes run fewer agents. The database upgrade (migration 0195) redraws Feature, Model change and Pipeline change where they are still as KOLONİ made them, without their Tester and general Reviewer steps: KOLONİ's own Checks step at a recipe's end already runs the tests and has a big or security-touching change reviewed. Feature is Write the spec → Build it → UI review; Model change keeps its plan, its experts' review, the build and Compare the metrics; Pipeline change is Change the pipeline → Check the data quality. The three check their tests and a review themselves, even in a project at Nothing. Your copies keep their steps: copy one again and add a step to have a Tester or a Reviewer of the recipe's own (see the Recipe cookbook). Going back to 0.1.9 draws them as before.
  • Codex's One run per model step is gone. A Codex provider saved so runs as One session per agent, and its window shows that. On a model server of your own a Codex session is not yet measured: try it there knowing that, or set How it runs to Native. See Set up a CLI.
  • The notification address is gone. The database upgrade (migration 0191) deletes a project's web hook address and the form it was posted in: notifications go to the desktop alone (Settings › This project › Notifications › Show a desktop notification, Windows only). A project whose day's summary went only to the address has its summary's time emptied: switch the desktop on and set the time again. Going back to 0.1.9 brings neither back. The copies in runtime/data/backups/ made before the upgrade and at the first start of a day still hold the address: delete them once you no longer need them if it is a secret.
  • The search by meaning is gone. The database upgrade (migration 0192) deletes the vectors it kept and the provider and model a project chose for it. Search deeper and an agent's recall match words, as before. Going back to 0.1.9 brings the setting back off.
  • Rooms are gone. At the first start the database is upgraded (migration 0193), after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/: open_room is taken off every role, your own too; what a room may spend is taken off every project and chat; each Room step of your saved recipes becomes an All at once group in its place, a step for each of its roles (its arrows and loops kept); a room left running is ended; and each turn that had a room gets one line in its chat, "A room was opened here. Rooms were removed in 0.1.10 …". The steps of a converted group are named "<the step's name> · ". The rooms themselves and what was said in them stay in the database, untouched. A recipe file in a folder's .koloni/recipes/ is read the same way as it loads, and is written in the new form the next time you save it. In a Room step that reviewed, a role that does the recipe's work at a step of its own works in the group and the others review - where every role does, all of them keep reviewing and the recipe says which "would check its own work": take that role out. A Room step that ran only if a problem was found now always runs. A saved recipe that no longer reads (a Room step near the 60-step limit, as a group past it) is listed under the recipes that are not read, with why. A room between teams no longer leaves its decision on the notebook or in .koloni/shared/decisions/; the files there stay readable, and teams agree through contracts/. For agents talking a question through, ask for a discussion; for work at the same time, sub-agents or a recipe's All at once step. To go back to 0.1.9, stop KOLONİ, return to 0.1.9 and put that copy back in place of runtime/data/koloni.db - a downgrade of the database alone gives the room budgets back at their defaults, not the values you had.

0.1.8¶

  • The database is upgraded at the first start (migrations 0176 to 0182), after KOLONİ copies it to runtime/data/backups/. Your chats and tasks are kept: To-do tasks become tasks of no story, a task left at work goes back to todo, and a Qoder provider is switched off, not removed. Only To-do's six Run settings are dropped (below). To go back to 0.1.7, stop KOLONİ and put that copy back in place of runtime/data/koloni.db.
  • To-do: Run the backlog, Runs by itself and Run settings are gone. Tick tasks on To-do and press Do in chat: they wait in that chat's queue and run there one by one, your own messages first. A project whose To-do ran by itself stops; the Run settings (level, model, who changes the files) are not kept anywhere. See Doing tasks in a chat.
  • A To-do task's turn is its chat's: at that chat's permission level and on its model (a CLI with tools of its own too), asking on its cards there - no longer under a night's rules. Look at the level of a chat before you give it tasks.
  • A story works on its own git branch (koloni/…) in a project in git: nothing of it is in your project until Close the story checks the branch and merges it or opens a pull request. See A story's branch.
  • ☾ Away runs its jobs at the level of the chat you go away from, no longer the lower of it and the To-do page's. See Work while you're away.
  • Copilot on your GitHub account: once the Models page or the chat's model panel opens, KOLONİ asks your account what it offers and switches off the models your plan does not list ("not on your Copilot plan now"). Look at the Copilot provider's Models tab. See Copilot on your GitHub account.
  • Copilot run Native, and Codex run Native with Sign-in: Its own for KOLONİ, now go on in their session from the topic's last turn. Press New topic for other work. See Going on from the last turn.
  • Some Always run rules cover less. A rule no longer covers ruff's or eslint's --fix past its words, nor another of their options that writes or loads code. A rule for a runner or tsc (uv run ruff, pip install -e ., npm install, npx tsc) no longer covers its line while an agent's write of a pyproject.toml, a lock (uv.lock, package-lock.json, …) or a tsconfig*.json in the project still stands, until your own edit or install replaces it. Below Full auto such a line asks on its card; in a trigger's turn it is not run. See What a rule never covers.
  • Work in a copy whose check by kind of work still fails (a page, a document, a config file, a script) is no longer taken in, at any level: press Take in anyway or Fix it. See What KOLONİ checked.
  • Kept working copies are now listed in Settings › The app › Your data. A trigger over its budget kept one at each run: discard what you no longer need.
  • Longest command: every project keeps 10 minutes. Raise it in Settings › This project › When you're asked › More (Commands and secrets before 0.1.10) where your tests or a build take longer.
  • Qoder is no longer run. A Qoder provider is kept, switched off for good, with its settings; its card says "no longer supported". If Qoder was your default provider (the first on the Models page), make another provider the default or delete Qoder: until then the chats, triggers and To-do turns that run on the default fail with that reason. A chat or a role set to Qoder fails the same way until you pick another model. See Qoder is no longer supported.
  • agy, and Codex run One run per model step, are offered only with Show experimental CLIs on (Settings › The app › Agents). A provider already set up so keeps working, tagged Experimental. See Experimental CLIs.

0.1.5¶

  • When you're away: a new project lets the Orchestrator decide a waiting card after 5 minutes. A project from before keeps what it had: turn it on under Settings › This project › When you're asked (Asking and away before 0.1.10). See Answering for you.
  • A harmful-looking command at Full auto stays Ask me, and Agents may delete what is disposable without asking stays off, in every project, until you turn them on there. See Check what I said first and Disposable deletes.
  • The "While you're away" card is gone: press ☾ Away on a chat's status line, or Go away on the Orchestrator's line. See Work while you're away.
  • Plan first is in the permission level's menu (or /plan); the button beside the level is gone.
  • No agent's window opens by itself any more: open it from the window icon on its box, a double click, Open window in the Inspector, or the Orchestrator's name over its turn.
  • GitHub Copilot CLI: KOLONİ runs the version Copilot itself reports, also after Copilot updated itself; from 1.0.114 on it runs Native again. See Set up a CLI.
  • A team's branch now starts from your folder as it is, your uncommitted files too, and its merge waits for your commit where the agent changed what you had not committed. Your repository's git hooks do not run for the agent's commits on its branch. See Branches.

0.1.2 to 0.1.4¶

Nothing to do.

0.1.1¶

  • A project that already had Project notes keeps them off for the Orchestrator: switch them on once on the Agents page, the Lead role, Knowledge tab. See Project notes.
  • The Orchestrator no longer stops at a fixed count of steps. A limit of your own goes on the Lead role. See Step limits.
