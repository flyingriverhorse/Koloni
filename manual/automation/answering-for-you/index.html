Answering for you¶

When you are away, questions can wait for you, or the Orchestrator can answer the safe ones itself.

Switching it on¶

Settings › This project › When you're asked › When you're away:

  • Everything waits for you;
  • The Orchestrator answers what it safely can, after a number of minutes (0 to 1440).

A new project has the second, after 5 minutes; a project from before 0.1.5 keeps what it had - turn it on here. In a chat with no folder everything waits for you. With it, once you have not answered a card for that many minutes, "the Orchestrator picks, says why, and you see it under "Decided for you"". In the chat you went away from (☾ Away) and its tasks' chats it decides at once, whatever this setting says - also the cards that waited when you pressed Go away. While you type an answer to a question, it waits (2 minutes after your last key); once you are back, a card held so waits this setting's minutes again.

"An Orchestrator on a CLI uses one request of your plan for each answer." When it answers, it also reads your preferences and this project's rules as saved on the Memory page.

What it may decide¶

Only:

  • an agent's question to you;
  • a recipe's "Ask me";
  • opening a sub-agent on its role's own model, while that provider has room;
  • going on or stopping at a sub-agent's step limit, where that card comes.

What always waits for you¶

Commands, file changes, the web, installing, secrets, folders, new roles or skills, deleting, Take in, Discard, Commit, a different model that may cost money, and Stop. It also does not answer when the turn is over its token limit, the project is over its monthly budget, or the provider is over its limit.

The same rule holds in To-do task chats and while you're away. Your Orchestrator's own questions to you wait for you - but in a job while you're away (☾ Away), where it decides them itself: "The user is away. Decide yourself and say what you assumed in one sentence. Only if the answer is something only the user can know (a password, an account, a personal fact), answer park." Its answer and reason are under Decided for you. What it answers park is parked for you under Waiting for you, and so is a question it could not answer (no answer it could read, a CLI past its most or with tools of its own); the job goes on. A sub-agent's question there is asked with the second sentence alone. park decides nothing by itself - an option spelled "Park" is parked, not picked. See Work while you're away.

Decided for you¶

What it answered shows under Decided for you on the Ready for you page, the project's whole list: who decided, what the Orchestrator chose, and why. Press OK to keep it, or Change → to give another answer while the agent that asked still works. If the answer cannot be undone any more, the row says why. The chat says it in one line at its top, "3 things were decided for you in this project and 2 wait for you here.", with Open in Ready for you. See Decisions. A chat with no folder keeps its own Decided for you while you were away box, with the same OK and Change →.

At Full auto the Orchestrator also answers questions in a chat you watch, at once, and some of KOLONİ's own cards, such as an agent's Continue / Stop here card at its step limit; those answers are in the same list. So does a command that Check what I said first ran without a card, or did not run, on a line of yours: "Coder ran … without asking" or "KOLONİ did not run … for Coder", with that line, until you press OK. Full auto alone does not let it open a sub-agent for you; of KOLONİ's own cards only the step limit's is decided while you're away. An Orchestrator on a CLI answers at Full auto only where When you're away is set to The Orchestrator answers what it safely can.

A decision the Orchestrator took without a card - a library, an approach - it can note for you with note_decision (0.1.11): it is in the same list, "Orchestrator · Decided: …" with its reason, until your OK; Change takes your own words. See Talking while agents work.

The same question again¶

If the same agent asks the same question again in the turn, with the same words and choices, it gets the Orchestrator's earlier answer at once, with no new request. Its line under Decided for you says it is "its answer when this was asked before". If you changed that answer, your answer is the one given.
