# Licenses

KOLONİ, the whole project (backend and frontend), is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public
License, version 3 or later** (AGPL-3.0-or-later): see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` in the repository.
`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md` says what the AGPL asks and how to ask for a commercial license, and
contributions come under the short `CLA.md`.

## What KOLONİ includes

`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` lists every third-party package KOLONİ ships, with its licence text: the
page's bundled packages, its fonts (IBM Plex, under the SIL Open Font License), the Python packages, and
other assets. It is generated by `scripts/notices.py` from `pyproject.toml`, `frontend/package-lock.json`
and the installed packages; run it again after `npm ci`. Its last section lists the packages whose
licences need a closer look.

Other included works:

- **The model catalogue** (`app/models_dev.json`), trimmed from models.dev, under the MIT licence
  (`app/models_dev.LICENSE`).
- **The draw.io viewer** (`frontend/public/drawio/`), unchanged, under the Apache License 2.0. KOLONİ's
  own script beside it keeps the viewer from loading anything from outside.
