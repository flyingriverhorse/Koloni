# A job while you're away

To-do tasks that run while you are away, for an hour or the night, each in its own copy of the
project, so nothing changes until you take it in.

## Before you go

1. In a chat with a folder, tell the Orchestrator you are going away and what to work on meanwhile.
   It puts each piece on your [To-do](../automation/todo.md) list and offers one line under its
   answer: "☾ Go away now?". The tasks run on this chat's model, at its permission level (the Away
   panel says which): below **Full auto**, a command no rule covers is parked for you - it does not
   run, and the task goes on without it.
2. Press **Change** on that line - or **☾ Away** on the chat's status line - to see the Away panel:
   the tasks from this chat are ticked; tick others from To-do too, and put them in order with
   **Move up** / **Move down**. Set when to stop (**Until** a time, or **spend at most** an amount) and
   what is kept out (files or folders no task may change). With neither limit set, the tasks run
   until they are done: there is no default spend limit, only your monthly budget if you set one.
3. Press **Go away**. The chat finishes its turn, then the tasks start one at a time. The status line
   says which, and how many cards were decided for you.

The Orchestrator decides for you, at once, what it may: an agent's question - in a task, its own too
-, a recipe's "Ask me", opening a sub-agent on its role's own model. What only you can know, and every
command's card, is parked for the morning; the task goes on. A change of a file that steers agents, the
web, a secret or a folder waits for you. Leave KOLONİ running: closing it ends Away.

## When you're back

1. Press **I'm back**, or write in the chat. The chat shows one summary: "While you were away (23:10 –
   07:42): 2 of 2 To-do done · 3 decided for you · 1 waits".
2. Under **Waiting for you**, **Run** or **Reject** each parked command and answer each parked question;
   once a task's are all given, what you said **Run** runs in its copy and its Orchestrator goes on in
   its chat. **Stop this task** closes the rest.
3. For a task that waits for a change, a web request or an agent to open, press **Allow and go on** or
   **Refuse**. For a folder or a secret, **Open its chat** and answer there.
4. **Review and take in** opens Ready for you; **On the canvas** opens the report. Look at each task's
   changes (**See the changes**), then **Take in** the ones you want, or **Take in all**.
5. **Try again** runs a task that could not be done, or was stopped, again as an ordinary turn.
6. **Proposed while you were away, not started** lists tasks the agents thought of; **Add to To-do**
   keeps them.
