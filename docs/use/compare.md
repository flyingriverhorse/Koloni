# Compare

**Compare…** under **More** (or `/compare`): "Send a message to two models side by side, and keep the
work of one".

1. Write the **Message**. The first model is the chat's own; choose the **Second model's provider** and its
   **Model name**.
2. Press **Compare**. Each model does the work itself, in its own copy of the project, one after the other.
3. Each side shows its time, tokens and cost, and **View diff** shows what it changed.
4. **Use *name*'s work** brings that side's changes into the project; the other side's are thrown away.
   Or **Discard both**: nothing reaches the project.

After you pick, **Undo these changes** takes the chosen work back.
