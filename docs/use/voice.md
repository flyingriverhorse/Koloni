# Voice

## Reading answers aloud

- **Read aloud** reads an answer; **Stop reading** stops.
- **Auto-read** reads each answer when the work ends: the **Whole answer**, or a **Short summary** ("A
  sentence or two the Orchestrator adds to be said").
- **How the Orchestrator speaks** (up to 300 characters) is sent with your message when Auto-read reads a
  short summary.

Answers are read with this computer's voices, or with Gemini's:

- **Gemini**: add a **Gemini API key** and **Save key**. The key stays in KOLONİ; the page never sees it
  again. "When off, this computer's voices read."

## Dictating

**Dictate**: "what you say is written into the box, and nothing is sent". **Dictation language** sets the
language you speak; it also sets the language answers are read in.

## Privacy

- "Speech recognition is your browser's: in Chrome and Edge what you say is sent to Google's or
  Microsoft's service to be written down."
- A computer voice marked online sends what it reads to its maker.
- "Text read with Gemini is sent to Google. On the free tier Google may use it to improve its products; on
  a paid key it does not."
- "KOLONİ keeps no sound and sends none anywhere."

Voice settings are kept in this browser: Settings › The app › **Voice**.
