**GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later** (AGPL-3.0-or-later): see
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE). That license is the same for everyone, and there is no separately
licensed part.

What the AGPL asks, in short (the license text is what counts):

- **Running KOLONİ** on your own machine, unmodified or modified, for yourself, needs nothing more.
- **Giving KOLONİ to others** (a copy, modified or not) must be under the AGPL, with its source.
- **Letting others use a modified KOLONİ over a network** means offering those users the
  Corresponding Source of your modified version, under the AGPL (section 13).

## When to ask for a commercial license

- **Commercial license exception:** you want to ship KOLONİ, or a modified KOLONİ, in a
  proprietary product or hosted service and cannot meet the AGPL's terms.
- **Enterprise support:** SLAs, roadmap commitments or long-term maintenance.
- **Custom licensing:** terms your legal or compliance team needs (indemnification, audit
  clauses, private amendments).
- **Partnerships:** co-marketing, joint roadmap planning or early access.

## How to start the conversation

1. Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/flyingriverhorse/Koloni/discussions) or
   an issue titled "Commercial license request", or
2. reach out through the contact channels in [`README.md`](README.md).

Please share your intended use, deployment model and any contractual requirements, so the right
agreement can be scoped.
